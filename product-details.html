Milk Pasteurizer

The EUP HTST pasteurizer delivers precise temperature control, regeneration efficiency, and sanitary design for continuous milk processing. Built in SS 304/316 with modular plate heat exchangers and optional PLC automation.

  • Capacity500 – 20,000 LPH
  • MaterialSS 304 / SS 316
  • TypeHTST / Batch
  • HeatingSteam / Hot Water
  • ControlManual / PLC-HMI
  • RegenerationUp to 90%
Get Quote WhatsApp

Key Features

Designed for dairy cooperatives and private processors who need reliable pasteurization with low energy cost and easy cleanability.

  • High regeneration ratio reducing steam demand
  • Hot water set with precise PID temperature control
  • Divert valve logic for under-pasteurized product
  • CIP-ready piping with sanitary fittings
  • Skid-mounted option for faster installation

Applications

Ideal for market milk, flavoured milk, yoghurt mix, ice cream mix base, and upstream processing for cheese and paneer plants.
