HTST vs Batch Pasteurization

Published Jun 12, 2026 · Process Engineering

Home/Blog/Article
Jun 12, 2026 Process 6 min read

Choosing between HTST (High Temperature Short Time) and batch pasteurization is one of the first process decisions for a growing dairy. Both achieve food safety — the right fit depends on volume, product variability, and utility costs.

When Batch Makes Sense

Batch pasteurizers are ideal for smaller dairies, specialty products, and operations that change recipes frequently. They require lower capital investment, simpler controls, and less floor space. Hold times are longer, so throughput is limited compared with continuous systems.

When HTST Wins

HTST systems excel above roughly 2,000–3,000 litres per hour. Regeneration sections recover heat from pasteurized milk to preheat incoming raw milk, cutting steam demand dramatically. Divert valves and holding tubes enforce process safety with minimal operator intervention.

Decision Checklist

Map daily and peak hourly volume, number of SKUs, available steam and chilled water, CIP philosophy, and expansion plans for the next five years. EUP engineers often recommend skid-mounted HTST with modular plate packs so capacity can grow without replacing the full frame.
